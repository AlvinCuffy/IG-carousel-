# Signal imagery — the Golden Horseshoe place library

The principle (from the LA reference): the background names the place so the headline doesn't have
to. Every slide that isn't an explainer cover sits on a real, recognisable Golden Horseshoe location or
object, photographed in monochrome with one warm light source.

## Treatment lock string

```
Shot on CineStill 800T, single warm tungsten source, charcoal-and-amber monochrome grade,
35mm lens, static tripod, layered foreground composition. Black-and-white photograph with
the only warmth coming from the light itself. Blurred foreground object, sharp subject,
soft background. Night or blue hour. No people's faces. No signage text. No colour.
```

Realism swap (Alex): for a "Prove It" or reader-submitted feel, replace the film stock with
`Shot on iPhone` and keep the rest.

- Keys on a windowsill
- A rent cheque and a pen on a kitchen table under a single lamp
- An LTB envelope on a hallway floor
- A landlord's clipboard on a stair rail
- Two doors on one landing (both-sides pieces)
- A duplex at night with two windows lit (both-sides pieces)

## What we do not do
- Colour photography. Blue sky. Sunsets.
- Stock-photo apartments with staged plants.
- Readable street signs or storefront names (they date and they clutter).
- Any face, tenant or landlord, in a real-case image.
- The raccoon anywhere near a real case.
