# The Bridge

The hook isn't manufactured tension — it's the most interesting thing you have to say, said first. Then bridge back to context. Applies to Reels, Shorts, cold opens, carousel slide 1, newsletter opens.

## Output structure
```
# The Bridge — [topic]
## The climax
## Climax-first openings (3)
## Short-bridge alternative
## Delete this
```

## Critical rules
- The climax is the TRUTH, not manufactured.
- Never use the artifacts the skill kills: "STOP scrolling", "you won't believe", "99% don't know", "wait for it".
- Always flag what to delete — the delete list is half the value.
- Match the format. A Reel open is one line.
- 3 openings minimum.
