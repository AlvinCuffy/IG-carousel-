# Goal Lock

ONE primary goal at a time: revenue, audience growth, audience quality, lead capture, or authority.

## Output
The goal · What you brought · On goal? (YES / PARTIALLY / NO) · The reasoning · The fix · The sharper version.

## Critical rules
- ONE goal. Force a priority.
- Be honest. Useless if it rubber-stamps.
- Never just judge — always give the fix or the sharper version.
- If the creator keeps bringing off-goal ideas, name the pattern.
