# Series Planner

## Output
The arc · 4-6 parts (angle, platform, format, connects to) · platform map · the payoff · start here.

## Critical rules
- The parts must CONNECT — a sequence that builds, not 5 posts on a topic.
- Map across platforms intentionally.
- The arc must build to a payoff.
- 4-6 parts.
- Name the starting part.
