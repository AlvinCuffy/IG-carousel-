# Long-to-Short

## Output
5-7 clippable moments, ranked: why it stands alone · script (climax-led opener, 3 beats, CTA) · source timestamp. Post this first. Sequencing across the week.

## Critical rules
- Each short must STAND ALONE.
- Climax-led openers.
- Cut weak moments. 5-7 max.
- Sequence them into a content week.
- Match the voice doc.
