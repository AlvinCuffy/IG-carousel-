# The 30-Day Instagram Growth System

Source: `/Grow with Alex/30day.docx`. Text extracted 2026-09-09. Condensed to the operating rules.

Built for creators and business owners who want Instagram as a lead generation engine. Every decision: does this move the needle towards clients and revenue?

## Before Day 1
A clear offer. A defined audience. 3–4 content pillars. Basic tools (Canva, CapCut, notes app, native analytics).

## Week 1 — Foundation & positioning
- **Competitor audit:** 8–10 monetising accounts. Document bio structure, formats, engagement, how they drive to offers, cadence. Save their top 10 Reels; find the 5–10x outliers. Look at saves and shares, not just views.
- **Content gap:** where are they generic? Your content says what they won't.
- **Bio:** Line 1 what you do + who for. Line 2 unique angle or proof. Line 3 CTA.
- **Visual identity:** 2–3 colours max, one or two fonts, real profile photo. Consistency beats complexity. If every post looks like it came from the same brain, you're winning.
- **3-format framework:** Reels (reach), carousels 5–8 slides (authority; frameworks, breakdowns; get saved and shared), Stories daily (trust, conversion).
- **Batch day:** 5–7 Reels, 2–3 carousels. Scripts first, film, then edit.

## Week 2 — Launch & engage
One Reel per day, one carousel every 2–3 days. Every Reel: hook in 1.5 s, value payoff, specific CTA. Hook structures: pattern interrupt, curiosity gap, authority opener, contrarian take. 15-minute engagement block daily. DMs are where deals happen.

## Week 3 — Analyse & double down

| Metric | Why | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Saves | Algorithm pushes high-value content | 3–5% of reach |
| Shares | Personal recommendation | 1–2% of reach |
| Profile visits | Curiosity about you | Growing weekly |
| Link clicks | Direct intent | Weekly trend |
| DM conversations | Warmest leads | 2–5 quality per week |
| Watch time | Structure working | 50%+ retention |

Take top 3 pieces, make 3–5 variations. Introduce your signature element: the recurring visual motif or breakdown nobody else does.

## Week 4 — Optimise & build the funnel
Lock the cadence (4–5 Reels, 2 carousels, daily stories). Algorithm signals: watch time and replays, sends weighted over likes, original over reposted, consistency. Lead magnet in link-in-bio. DM funnel via Stories ("DIY or DFY").

## Daily rhythm (45 min)
5 analytics · 5 replies · 10 post · 10 engage on 5–10 accounts · 5 stories · 10 save competitor posts for research.
