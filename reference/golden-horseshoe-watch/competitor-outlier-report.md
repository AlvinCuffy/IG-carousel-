# Golden Horseshoe Watch — Competitor & Outlier Content Report

Source: Dropbox `/Grow with Alex/The Golden Horseshoe Watch/golden-horseshoe-watch-—-competitor-outlier-content-report.pdf` (2026-09-09).

## Executive summary
- **Format wins, not niche.** Strongest predictor of outlier performance: checklist infographics, raw proof-documentation clips, and watchdog callout formats. Talking-head opinion and static real-estate photos underperform.
- **Carousels are dead in this space** (zero found across @gideonthegavel, @sweetchildmontse, @claimswithelle, @adamsellsnj). All growth is reel/TikTok video. *(Note: the idea bank keeps carousels as a secondary, saves-tracked format — the two documents disagree deliberately.)*
- **@gideonthegavel is the best template**, but watch the pivot into general "know your rights" legal content — proof the checklist format transfers across legal sub-niches.
- **One-hit accounts are a trap.** @getailixy's 25.9M-view fluke (2,831x median) sits on a 1K–9K steady state.
