# Golden Horseshoe Watch — Research Brief (vidIQ)

Source: Dropbox `/Grow with Alex/The Golden Horseshoe Watch/golden-horseshoe-watch-vidiq-brief.md` + the two PDF revisions (2026-09-09). This is the account's own positioning document.

## Who we are
- **Account:** @goldenhorseshoewatch (Instagram, Creator account). Name: Golden Horseshoe Watch.
- **What it is:** a non-official, awareness-first watchdog account covering tenant, landlord, and consumer-rights issues across the Golden Horseshoe region of Ontario (Brampton → Mississauga → Hamilton, expanding city by city).
- **What it is NOT:** not a law firm, not an official legal-advice source, not branded to any company. Facts only, plus a call to action.
- **Voice:** "Watchdog opens, Neighbor closes." Sharp tenant/landlord facts up front, warm and human close. Never corporate, never salesy.
- **Mascot / visual identity:** a raccoon detective — the "watcher" of the neighbourhood — holding a horseshoe-shaped magnifying glass. Palette: charcoal/near-black background, off-white primary text, amber gold accent used sparingly. Real photographic depth (foreground/subject/background layering) over flat graphics — content that looks shot, not just designed.

## Who we help
Renters and tenants in the Golden Horseshoe dealing with landlord issues: unaddressed repairs, illegal entry, utility shutoffs, lock changes, wrongful eviction threats, unfair rent increases, RTA/LTB violations. Secondary: small consumers who could use accessible legal help.

## Where we lead people
Content → a hand-raise (comment, share, tag, or DM the word **WATCH**) → a private conversation/Zoom → a $50/month LegalShield membership with 24/7 lawyer access. Never sells directly in-feed.

## Testing philosophy
Alex Garcia (House of AG) high-volume testing. Metrics: skip rate under 50% at 3 s; watch time past the 12-second death zone; ~2 likes per 1 share.

Strong formats identified: animated/looping checklist reels; POV/observational walkthroughs; rapid-fire Q&A talking-head.

Reference accounts: @gideonthegavel, @saberisixrealestate, @oguilhermeoliveira.adv, @real.with.sonia, @adamsellsnj.

## The angle has two sides
Bill 60 (effective Sept 21, 2026) cuts both ways: N4 non-payment notice period drops 14 → 7 days (favours landlords); N12 personal-use evictions tighten — named person must move in within 60 days or bad-faith presumption (favours tenants). Both are valid leads.

## Geographic sourcing rule
- **Style, format, creative inspiration** — from anywhere.
- **Statistics and data in posts** — Ontario or Canadian federal sources only (Tribunals Ontario, Ontario LTB, CMHC, Statistics Canada).
- **Real stories and cases in posts** — genuinely from the Golden Horseshoe (Brampton, Mississauga, Hamilton, expanding).

Borrow the "how" from anywhere, keep the "what" strictly Ontario/Golden Horseshoe.

## Cover style by content type (working assumption)
Mascot/branded cover for our own findings, data, explainers and checklists. Real, unbranded presentation for real people's real stories — the mascot on a real case breaks "this really happened" credibility.
